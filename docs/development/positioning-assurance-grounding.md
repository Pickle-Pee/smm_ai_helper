# POSITIONING assurance and qualification boundary

Manual review of a complete synthetic workshop graph on cf81ebb found two
unsupported additions outside RTB: a guaranteed finished product and an
experienced master. The source supplied a master and collection after firing;
neither source presence nor an INFERENCE label authorized stronger promises.
Three regressions reproduced acceptance on the former code.

Across every newly generated POSITIONING statement, a finite RU/EN set of
guarantee and qualification words now requires the WHOLE statement as a literal
contiguous quote from an actually cited local product_truth/existing_proof value.
Matching words alone, uncited sources, other context categories and parents do
not authorize it. Kind does not bypass the check. Generation instructions ask
for neutral wording and retention of fulfillment conditions. Unsupported output
fails with bounded product_assurance_support_required metadata, without repair,
automatic retry or artifact persistence. Existing numeric/product/RTB rules keep
their precedence. Source text and model response remain unmodified.

This deliberately conservative lexical boundary also covers negated uses of its
words; it is not a negation parser or general semantic entailment checker. It
does not detect every possible unsupported adjective or promise. Literal quotes
establish source presence, not independent verification, and can omit context.
Manual business review remains necessary. Other creative hypotheses and
recommendations remain available. Old saved artifacts stay readable and are not
retroactively certified. DTOs, schema, flags, source IDs, QG and budgets are
unchanged; no migration.
